🎯 HOW USERS INSTALL:

   📱 ANDROID:
      1. Open in Chrome/Firefox
      2. Tap "Install" or Menu → "Add to Home Screen"
      3. Confirm
      4. Icon on home screen ✅

   🍎 iOS:
      1. Open in Safari (required)
      2. Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
      3. Enter name
      4. Tap "Add"
      5. Icon on home screen ✅

      1. Open in Chrome or Edge

      3. Confirm
      4. App in Start Menu ✅

   🖥️  MAC:
      1. Open in Safari or Chrome
      2. Click install in menu
      3. Confirm
      4. App in Applications ✅
