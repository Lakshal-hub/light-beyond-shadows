Understanding Corruption

  • Corruption is a broad, it's a complex phenomenon where and individuals or institution abuse their authority or position, for personal gain often at the expense of the public good.
  • It includes embezzlement of public funds, nepotism, favoring family's, relatives, and friend for jobs, kickbaks, and abuse of discretion in decision making.
  • Corruption can occur at every level of society specially in local governments, officials accepting money to skip building safety checks, to high ranking leaders siphoning off millions and billions from national development budgets, even in private companies bribing officials to win lucrative contracts.
  • Corruption breaks the trust of people and their government, and diverts resources away from those who need them most.
